_Lightweeds
2006
https://www.moma.org/collection/works/110255?sov_referrer=art_term&art_term_slug=interactive-design

_Lightweeds_ are **living digital organisms**. They grow indoors but depend on sunshine, rainfall, and wind measured in the outside world through sensors placed on the outer walls of the building (**these Lightweeds are responding to information captured by sensors just outside the Museum.**) As human traffic—yes, you—goes by, they bend, lose their seeds, and pollinate other walls throughout the space in a constantly evolving wallpaper that reflects the character of the space and its use.
